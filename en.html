About me

Hi, I’m Yessica!

I’m a graphic designer specialising in branding, photography and visual content creation. I love turning ideas into brand identities with personality, caring for every detail to build visual worlds that convey emotion, authenticity and value.

I mainly work with hotels, restaurants and wellness businesses, supporting both entrepreneurs starting a new project and established brands looking to evolve.

I believe in the power of a good image, in the small details and in design’s ability to tell stories. My goal is for every project to have its own identity, communicate what makes it special and connect with the right people.

Yessica Diaz

How we work

My process

  1. 01

    We book a call

    A free 30-minute call to talk about your project, understand your needs and find out how I can help you build your brand.

    Book your call →
  2. 02

    We choose the right package

    I help you choose the service that best fits your brand’s needs and goals. Once chosen, we move on to the quote.

  3. 03

    Tailored quote

    With the information from the form, I prepare a tailored quote for your project. If you’re happy to work with me, we move on to the contract.

  4. 04

    The contract

    To work together we sign a contract with all the project details: what you’ve booked, the terms and the payments, among other things.

  5. 05

    Creative phase

    My method is collaborative: you’ll be involved throughout the process and kept up to date at every stage.

    • Creating the brief
    • First sketches
    • Sending proposals
  6. 06

    Revisions and feedback

    I want you to be happy with the result. We review every deliverable and, if something doesn’t feel right, we adjust it and go through feedback again.

Before we start

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask me before we work together. If your question isn’t here, just write to me.

How long does each service take?

The audit is delivered in 5 to 7 working days. Visual identity and web projects are done in phases, with a fixed schedule from the start. Monthly content packages have a minimum commitment of 3 months.

How do payments work?

For one-off projects, 50% upfront and the rest on delivery. Monthly packages are paid in advance each month. Everything is set out in writing in the proposal.

Do you work with international brands?

Yes. I’m based in Spain and work remotely with brands from any country; I’ve also worked with clients in Latin America. For on-site photo sessions, we plan around the location.

Can I customise the packages?

Of course. The packages are a starting point: I can adjust the number of pieces, add photography or combine them to suit your business.

What do I need to get started?

A first conversation to get to know your business and goals, and commitment to the process. Before we start, I’ll send you a questionnaire to get to know your brand in depth.
